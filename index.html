ABOUT THE PROJECT

TRAIN WITH PURPOSE.

FitForge is a fictional personal training brand created as a professional website concept.

The project explores how a modern fitness brand can combine strong visual identity, clear information architecture and conversion-focused user experience.

Every section was designed with responsive layouts and real-world business presentation in mind.
