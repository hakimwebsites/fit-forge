ABOUT THE PROJECT

TRAIN WITH PURPOSE.

FitForge is a fictional personal training brand created to demonstrate how a premium fitness business could present its services online.

The concept combines strong typography, a high-contrast visual identity and conversion-focused layouts to create a website that feels energetic without becoming visually overwhelming.

TYPE Website Concept
FOCUS Fitness / Personal Training
STYLE Dark / Minimal / Bold

DESIGN APPROACH

MORE THAN A WORKOUT.

The concept was designed around four principles that help a service-based website feel professional.

01

Strong Visual Identity

A bold black-and-lime palette creates a recognizable visual direction suitable for a modern fitness brand.

02

Clear Information

Content is broken into focused sections so visitors can quickly understand the service and value.

03

Human Experience

Simple interactions, natural spacing and realistic content keep the interface from feeling over-designed.

04

Easy Contact

WhatsApp and email contact points make it easy for visitors to take the next step.
